Install the Jeep T3

You only do this once. After that the T3 updates itself over Wi-Fi whenever the Jeep is stopped.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on the PC.
  2. Plug the T3 into the PC with its USB cable.
  3. Click Install, pick the T3's COM port, and say yes to erasing it.
  4. When it says done, unplug it and put it in the Jeep. Then use Heep's T3 setup.
This browser can't do it: open this page in Chrome or Edge on the PC. Open this page from its https:// address.

If no COM port shows up, the PC may need the USB driver for the T3's CH9102 or CP2104 chip.
